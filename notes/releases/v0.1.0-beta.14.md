Home now offers more ways to resume reading and explore your existing library.

- Reading shelves start with 24 records and load more independently. Continue reading shows multiple cards, includes local and MangaDex progress, and opens the appropriate unread chapter directly.
- **Rediscover** offers stable shuffled unread picks, including a fallback for recently imported libraries. **Quick reads** highlights unread oneshots and finished works with up to 200 known local pages, excluding known missing chapters.
- Pin a list or saved Manga/Comic filter to Home. **Customize Home** hides and reorders shelves, selects discovery media, and scopes updates to your reading list and favorites.
- Compact statistics and horizontal paging keep the page manageable. Returning from a work preserves loaded shelf depth; background completion events defer refreshes while Home is offscreen.
- Recent updates include cached MangaDex publication dates and avoid duplicating downloaded provider chapters.

No database migration or manual configuration is required. Reading progress and saved worker settings carry over; Home layout preferences are saved in your browser.
