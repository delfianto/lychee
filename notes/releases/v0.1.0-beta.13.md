Finding your next read and following background work are more predictable in this beta.

- Download and sync lists put active work first, then queued work, with natural title/folder ordering and numeric chapter ordering. Downloads select the lowest queued chapter even when a bulk insert gives every entry the same timestamp. Shared chapter/fetch admission is FIFO, while completed files can still arrive out of order during parallel processing.
- Gallery scans, cover generation, and thumbnail preparation follow natural folder/title order instead of filesystem enumeration order.
- Demographics, content ratings, publication statuses, and tags on a manga or comic detail page open the matching library filter directly. Comic links stay in Comics, taxonomy IDs survive display-label changes, and selected filters appear as removable chips.
- Removed the unused user-avatar placeholder from the navigation bar.

Existing libraries, reading progress, and worker preferences carry over. No new database migration or manual configuration is required. Interrupted chapter downloads resume after restart.
