Chapter storage and background activity are easier to manage in this beta.

- Chapters without a volume, including unvolumed oneshots, download directly into the manga UUID folder. Later syncs move managed archives when volume assignments change and flatten existing `No Volume/` folders, preserving chapter IDs and reading progress without re-encoding.
- Downloads and Sync have independent, persistent **Auto-clear completed** toggles. Successful entries clear after processing finishes; failed and cancelled entries remain available for inspection.
- The activity badge counts queued/processing chapters and individual gallery folders through their final thumbnail. Reconnect recovery removes stale tasks, and large syncs keep every active job.

The database upgrade runs automatically and preserves saved worker settings. Both auto-clear toggles start off.
